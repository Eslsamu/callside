# Community conduct

Callside welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Keep discussion focused on the work and make participation practical for others.

- Give specific, constructive feedback and respect differing technical opinions.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or publish private information about anyone.
- Do not post credentials, private conversations, or recordings without permission.
- Respect maintainers' and contributors' time. Reproducible reports and small, focused changes help.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Enforcement should be proportionate and based on behavior.

Use GitHub's **Report content** option for abuse. When a maintainer provides a private contact method on their profile, you may also use it for conduct concerns. Do not post private incident details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
